Sample check method

methods/sample-check.md · Edition a5a7eee of avd-assess, 2026-10-21. What changed

A short, point-in-time health check of an estate's measured user experience: a few chosen hours, judged against thresholds that each carry their source, before anyone changes anything. It sits between the assessment, which judges configuration, and the targets and benchmarks, which say what good looks like. No open method covers it, so most of it is ours ([G1]).

Method

  1. Collect the counters Insights recommends for session hosts, at the intervals it lists, so the check reads data an estate may already have ([1, perf-counters]).
  2. Sample at least three working days and four one-hour windows a day, spread over the week and over the working hours from intake, rather than a single busy afternoon ([G1], [2, patterns-over-days], [G2]).
  3. Treat an hour as a data point, not a finding: a host is over a threshold only when 5% of its sampled hours are, and severity comes from the share of hosts over ([G1]).
  4. Take every threshold from the gold-standard registry and print its citation with the result ([G3]): round-trip time ([3, rtt-under-150]), input delay ([1, input-delay-bands], [4, input-delay-host-100]), CPU ([2, cpu-bands]) and CPU queue per core ([2, pql-per-processor]).
  5. Where nothing is published, record a baseline instead of a verdict: memory, disk latency, application load and logon time start as the estate's own median and 95th percentile ([G4], [G1]).
  6. Give each metric an assessment outcome: pass, warn, gap or unknown; too little coverage is unknown, never a pass ([G1], [G5]).
  7. Keep the evidence, not the people: windows, coverage, hosts over and their share of hours, with hosts as pseudonyms only ([G1]).
  8. Feed the results forward: outcomes count as performance-pillar checks in the score, and baselines become the estate's baseline benchmarks ([G5], [G4]).
  9. Repeat with the same days and windows after a change and compare, so before and after are measured the same way ([5, milestones]).
  10. Send disagreements back: a threshold that real runs keep contradicting becomes a disagrees observation and can demote its registry entry ([G3]).

Not yet known

  • No sample check has run against real users yet; every threshold except round-trip time is still a candidate in the registry ([G3]).
  • The per-hour share this method needs isn't in avd-second-brain's C12 forensic facts yet; until it is, producers judge from the 95th percentile and say so ([G1]).
