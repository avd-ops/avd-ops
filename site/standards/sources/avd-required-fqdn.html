#blocking-unsupported published

Deployments where the listed session host FQDNs and endpoints are blocked are not supported.

#host-list published

Session hosts need outbound access to a published list of FQDNs, one UDP range (relayed RDP on 3478) and certificate endpoints on TCP 80; no inbound port is needed.

✓ mapped○ measurable○ validated

Cited by Network method. Cite: avd-assess@a5a7eee:references/avd-required-fqdn.yaml#host-list

#client-list published

End-user devices need outbound access to their own published list; blocking it is not supported.

✓ mapped○ measurable○ validated
