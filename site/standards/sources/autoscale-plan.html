Create and assign an autoscale scaling plan for Azure Virtual Desktop

references/autoscale-plan.yaml · Edition cfcc302 of avd-assess, 2026-10-27. What changed

Microsoft Learn. Create and assign an autoscale scaling plan for Azure Virtual Desktop. Pinned at git_commit_id 9482be6, updated 2026-08-31; read 2026-10-21. Microsoft Learn content (link only). We keep one line in our own words per claim and link the source; its text stays with its publisher. The prerequisites and the meaning of each schedule field. The concepts page (ref:avd-autoscale) says what autoscale is for; this one says what a correct plan needs. Its subscription-scope rule for the power role is contested by ref:troubleshoot-autoscale#role-scope-containing-pool (obs:power-role-at-resource-group-scope).
