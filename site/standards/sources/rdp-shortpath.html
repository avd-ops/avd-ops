#udp-transport published

RDP Shortpath sets up a UDP transport between client and host, with more reliable connections and more consistent latency; TCP reverse connect stays the fallback.

Every connection starts on TCP reverse connect through the gateway, then tries UDP in parallel; if UDP succeeds the TCP connection is dropped, otherwise TCP stays.
