Brand and design system

One quiet, precise look for every avd-ops page and portal. Everything here is drawn with the live stylesheets: switch the theme in the bar and the values, contrast and examples follow.

Principles

  • Deploy quietlyThe interface stays out of the way of the work. Hairlines instead of shadows, one accent, and nothing that moves unless a person moved it.
  • Bottom line firstEach screen opens with what to do or what happened, in one plain sentence. Detail waits behind "more".
  • Precision you can checkEvery color is a token with a light and a dark value that pass WCAG AA, every font is served from this site, and the tests hold both.
  • State in words and shapeResults and maturity always carry a word and a shape; color only repeats them.

Pick a swatch to copy its token. Each shows its value in the current theme and, for anything text sits on or is set in, its contrast against the page.

Diagrams

One language for every drawing, on the page and in the exported files: solid boxes are built pieces, dashed ones optional or outside, the accent marks the core path, a heavy ground is the platform, on a faint drafting grid.

Logo

The mark, and the lockup with the tagline for places outside the bar: light, dark and one color. The text in the lockups is outlined, so they look the same without the fonts installed. Keep clear space of a quarter of the mark's height, and never redraw or recolor it.

Every brand file (avatars, PNG exports, link preview images, the original concept) is in brand/, with profile.json, the whole brand in one file for tools and Claude.
