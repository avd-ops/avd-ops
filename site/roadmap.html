Roadmap

Backlog and status across the avd-ops project.

Where each of the 19 repositories stands today, what is in its backlog, and what is being built, explored and shipped. Direction, not a promise. No dates.

Every board item below is a public GitHub issue approved with the roadmap and accepted labels, plus a status:* label. Snapshot as of 2026-11-09.
