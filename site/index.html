Enterprise guardrails

  • Read first, approve changesCollectors and agents read freely; every estate change requires human approval and a kill switch.
  • Tenant data stays with the tenantPublic repos hold generalized patterns only. Estate facts stay in your own private repository.
  • Every number traces to evidenceSizing and rules carry a reason, measured observations, and commit-level citations.
